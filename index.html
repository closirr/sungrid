SUNGRID
Feed the sun. Chain the lasers.
v0.5

HOW TO PLAY

SUN ATOMS ARE EVERYTHING

Solar Panels emit energy packets every 2s. Packets fly to whatever is in range — relays pass them on, construction sites and lasers consume them. No packets, no base.

BUILD

Pick a tool (1–5), click to place. Placement costs resources up front, then the site eats energy packets until finished. Buildings can go anywhere — keep them from overlapping.

CHAIN LASERS

With the Select tool, drag from one Laser to another: the feeder stops shooting and pours its beam into the receiver. Every feeder adds +1 damage and +20% range. Feeders burn their own charge while the chain fires.

MINE MINERALS

Harvesters near minerals (green radius) convert energy packets into resources: 1 charge = 1 mineral = 1 R$. Relays heat up — over 100 heat, packets flying through are lost.

SURVIVE

UFO waves grow every 10 seconds. UFOs ram your buildings and knock them around. Lose every building and it's over.

PAUSED

BASE DESTROYED

Everything is gone.
