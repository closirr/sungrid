HOW TO PLAY

SUN ATOMS ARE POWER

Power Plants generate energy. Sun atoms flow along your Energy Links as glowing pellets — every building must touch the grid to work.

CHAIN YOUR LASERS

Click LINK on a Laser and aim it at another Laser: the receiver gains +50% damage and +25% range per feeder. Chains of 5–10 become death beams — but each feeder stops shooting on its own.

DON'T OVERLOAD THE GRID

Links carry limited energy per second. Too little copper or too much production overheats them — at full heat a link burns out and explodes.

MINE, BUILD, DEFEND

Harvesters on minerals dig credits. Energy controls SPEED: supplied buildings build, fire and repair faster. Enemies march from the gates — defend the Core!
