HOW TO PLAY

DEFEND THE CORE

Monsters attack in waves from the map edges. If they reach your Core, it takes damage. At 0% you lose.

CHAIN PRISMS

Prisms beam energy into Lasers. Each Prism in a chain multiplies the Laser's damage. Prism → Prism → Laser builds a superweapon that pierces enemies.

MAZE THEM

Walls redirect monsters. Make them walk the long way through your killzone — they will chew walls if fully blocked.

EXPAND FOR POWER

Extractors on crystals generate energy. Buildings must be inside your power network — use Pylons to reach distant crystals.
